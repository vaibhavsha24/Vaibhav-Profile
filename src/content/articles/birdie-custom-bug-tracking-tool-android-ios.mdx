Before **Birdie**, internal and beta bug reporting at Probo flowed primarily through WhatsApp threads. While chat made it easy for anyone to drop a screenshot, it created fragmented communication, stripped away essential technical context, and caused slow, manual back-and-forth during triage.

To eliminate that friction across mobile releases, Probo built **Birdie**—an in-house bug reporting and diagnostics tool embedded directly into the Android and iOS applications.

## Why Chat-Based Bug Reporting Breaks Down

When bugs arrive as ad-hoc screenshots inside messaging apps:

- Engineers lack **app version, OS version, and device model** context without asking follow-up questions.
- Duplicate reports and unresolved threads bury critical regressions in noisy chat history.
- Reporters have no visibility into whether an issue has been triaged, assigned, or resolved.

## How the Birdie Workflow Operates

Birdie moved the entire reporting and diagnostic capture loop inside the application itself:

1. **In-App Trigger & Screenshot Capture**: Users report issues directly from inside the app using screenshot-based bug capture.
2. **Optional Context & Permissions**: Reporters can attach optional descriptions while the SDK handles required device permissions cleanly.
3. **Automatic Technical Metadata**: Every submission automatically bundles the **app version, device model, and OS version**, removing manual guesswork.
4. **Dedicated Slack Routing**: Reports flow straight into a dedicated Slack channel for immediate engineering visibility.
5. **Kanban Triage & Status Updates**: Issues are categorized and tracked on a Kanban workflow, with status updates surfaced back inside the app.

## Impact on Mobile Engineering Velocity

Embedding diagnostics into the mobile client turned bug reporting from fragmented chat messages into actionable, reproducible tickets—shortening triage cycles and tightening the feedback loop across product, QA, and Android/iOS engineering.
